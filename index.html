THE PROBLEM

Campus opportunities are easy to overlook when updates are scattered.

Students currently need to check different pages, chats, posters, and announcements. Important event details can be inconsistent or difficult to find at the right time.

Why students should care:

One trusted event hub saves time and makes it easier to take part in academic, career, cultural, sports, wellness, and community activities.

THE SOLUTION

One simple workflow from discovery to feedback.

The landing page reflects features demonstrated by the current HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and localStorage prototype.
