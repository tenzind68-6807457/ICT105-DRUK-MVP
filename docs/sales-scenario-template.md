# Smart Campus Event Hub Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title

**Smart Campus Event Hub — Discover, Register, and Manage Campus Events**

---

## 2. Target User and Customer

- **Primary users:** First-year undergraduate students and international or exchange students at Rangsit University who are unfamiliar with university communication channels and campus activities.
- **Secondary users:** Student clubs, event organizers, and campus administrators who create, manage, and promote campus events.
- **Operational stakeholder:** A Smart Campus Event Hub administrator who manages event information, approves updates, monitors registrations, and ensures event details remain accurate.
- **Potential adoption partners:** Student Affairs Office, International College, Student Clubs, Orientation Leaders, and university departments that regularly organize campus activities.

---

## 3. Realistic Situation

Sarah is a first-year international student who has recently started studying at Rangsit University. She wants to participate in workshops, sports competitions, volunteer programs, and student club activities to make friends and gain university experience. However, event announcements are spread across Facebook pages, LINE groups, posters, university websites, and student club announcements. She often discovers events after registration has closed or misses them completely because the information is scattered across multiple platforms.

During the university orientation program, a student ambassador introduces the **Smart Campus Event Hub** and explains that it provides one centralized platform for discovering campus events. Sarah opens the landing page, clicks **Try Demo**, browses upcoming events, searches for a workshop using keywords, views detailed event information, registers for the event, and later checks her participation status through the **My Events Dashboard**. After attending the event, she submits a rating and review to share her experience with other students.

---

## 4. Pain Point

Students currently experience several challenges when trying to participate in campus events:

- Event information is scattered across multiple communication channels.
- Students miss registration deadlines because announcements are difficult to find.
- Important event information such as venue, organizer, date, and available seats is not always available in one place.
- Students have difficulty tracking which events they have already registered for.
- Event organizers lack a centralized platform to publish and manage campus activities efficiently.

---

## 5. Product Introduction

The **Smart Campus Event Hub** is introduced through its responsive landing page with the message:

> **"Discover, register, and manage campus events—all in one place."**

The landing page explains the problem of scattered event information and demonstrates how the platform simplifies event discovery and participation. Users can access the working prototype through the **Try Demo** button, where they can browse events, search and filter activities, register for events, manage their registrations through the **My Events Dashboard**, and submit reviews after completing an event.

The current version is an educational MVP prototype built using **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and browser localStorage**. It uses sample data and simulated user accounts for demonstration purposes and does not connect to a live university database.

---

## 6. Value Message

> **For first-year undergraduate students and international or exchange students who experience difficulty finding campus events because information is scattered across multiple communication channels, our Smart Campus Event Hub helps them discover, register for, and manage campus events by providing a centralized platform with event browsing, search and filtering, detailed event information, event registration, participation tracking, and administrator event management.**

---

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment

**Student Ambassador:**

*"Have you ever missed an interesting campus event because you found the announcement too late or couldn't find all the information?"*

**Student:**

*"Yes. I usually see events after registration has already closed, or I have to search through several different groups."*

**Student Ambassador:**

*"That's exactly why we developed the Smart Campus Event Hub. Instead of checking multiple social media pages, messaging groups, and posters, you can browse all campus events in one place. You can search for events, filter them by category, view complete event details, register online, and keep track of your registered events through your personal dashboard."*

The ambassador opens the landing page, clicks **Try Demo**, browses available events, searches for a workshop, opens the event details page, and registers for the event using the prototype.

**Student Ambassador:**

*"After attending an event, you can return to your dashboard to submit a review and rating. Event organizers and administrators can also manage event information through the Admin Dashboard, making it easier to keep students informed."*

**Student:**

*"This would definitely make it easier to discover events and avoid missing registration deadlines."*

**Student Ambassador:**

*"Please try the demo and let us know if anything could be improved. Your feedback will help us enhance the final version of the Smart Campus Event Hub."*

---

## 8. Expected User Action

After viewing the demonstration, users should:

1. Select **Try Demo** from the landing page.
2. Browse available campus events.
3. Search and filter events using keywords or categories.
4. View complete event details.
5. Register for an event.
6. View registered events through the **My Events Dashboard**.
7. Submit a review after attending a completed event.
8. Provide usability feedback and recommend the platform to other students.

The classroom demonstration uses sample data and simulated user accounts stored in browser localStorage.

---

## 9. Value for Stakeholders

### Students

- Discover campus events more easily.
- Reduce the chance of missing important activities.
- Track registrations in one dashboard.
- Share feedback after participating in events.

### Student Clubs and Event Organizers

- Publish campus events through one centralized platform.
- Reach more students efficiently.
- Keep event information organized and up to date.

### Campus Administrators

- Manage campus events from one administrative dashboard.
- Maintain accurate event information.
- Support student engagement across the university.

### Project Team

- Collect usability feedback.
- Measure user engagement and registration behavior.
- Validate whether the prototype solves the problem of scattered campus event information before the final MVP submission.

## 11. Success Signal

The sales scenario is successful if a first-year or international student can understand the purpose of the Smart Campus Event Hub, successfully browse campus events, search for activities, view event details, complete an event registration, access the My Events Dashboard, understand that event reviews become available after completing an event, and recognize that the current version is an educational web prototype using browser localStorage rather than a live university system.
