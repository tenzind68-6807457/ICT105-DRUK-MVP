# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name

**Smart Campus Event Hub**

---

## Target Early Users

The first users of the Smart Campus Event Hub prototype are:

- First-year undergraduate students at Rangsit University
- International and exchange students
- Student clubs and organizations
- Campus event organizers

These users frequently need a centralized platform to discover, register for, and manage campus events.

---

## Main Value Proposition

**Discover, register for, and stay updated with all campus events through one centralized and easy-to-use platform.**

---

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| University LINE / Class Chat Groups | First-year and international students | Never miss campus events again | Open prototype link | Demo clicks and prototype visits | Students actively communicate through class chat groups |
| QR Code on Campus Posters | Students on campus | Scan to discover upcoming campus events | Scan QR code and browse events | QR scans and homepage visits | Students frequently pass campus notice boards |
| Instagram Story / Facebook Post | Student community | Find workshops, competitions, and campus activities in one place | Visit prototype and explore events | Story clicks and link visits | Popular social media platforms among students |
| Student Club Announcements | Club members | Register for campus events faster | Open prototype and register | Event registrations | Clubs regularly promote activities to students |
| University Orientation Sessions | New students | Start your university journey by exploring campus events | Visit homepage and create an account | New user registrations | Orientation introduces students to campus services |

---

## Launch Sequence

1. Prepare the Smart Campus Event Hub prototype and GitHub Pages link.
2. Generate QR codes linking to the prototype.
3. Share the prototype through class chat groups, student clubs, and social media.
4. Encourage students to browse events, register, and save favorite events.
5. Record homepage visits, event registrations, favorites, and search usage.
6. Collect feedback through surveys and usability testing.
7. Improve the interface, search/filter features, and event management based on user feedback.
8. Update project documentation, startup metrics, and GitHub repository.

---

## Risk and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Students may ignore the prototype link. | Use attractive posters, QR codes, and social media promotions with clear benefits. |
| Low participation during testing. | Invite classmates, first-year students, and international students to participate during lectures or orientation activities. |
| Users may experience confusion when searching or registering for events. | Improve navigation, search filters, and provide clearer instructions throughout the prototype. |
| Prototype limitations due to LocalStorage. | Explain that the current version is an MVP demonstration and future versions will include a real database and backend services. |
| Feedback may not represent all users. | Collect responses from different student groups, including first-year, international students, organizers, and campus administrators. |
